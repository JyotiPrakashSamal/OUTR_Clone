BTech IT

Bachelor of Technology in Information Technology

The B.Tech program in Information Technology builds a strong foundation in modern computing, software development, database systems, and networking.

  • Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
  • Core programming and systems design
  • Hands-on projects and industry internships
  • Preparation for top IT industry roles

BTech CSC

Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science & Engineering

This program focuses on the core principles of computer science, covering algorithms, artificial intelligence, operating systems, and computer architecture.

  • Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
  • In-depth study of computer science theories
  • Focus on emerging tech like AI/ML
  • Excellent placement opportunities

M.Plan in Urban and Regional Planning

Master of Planning in Urban and Regional Planning

The M.Plan program focuses on urban development, regional planning, sustainable infrastructure, transportation systems, and smart city planning for modern urban environments.

  • Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  • Focus on urban and regional development
  • Planning and sustainable infrastructure studies
  • Fieldwork and project-based learning

M.Tech in Geotechnical Engineering

Master of Technology in Geotechnical Engineering

This program provides advanced knowledge in soil mechanics, foundation engineering, rock mechanics, ground improvement techniques, and geotechnical analysis.

  • Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  • Advanced study of soil and foundation engineering
  • Laboratory and field investigation training
  • Research-oriented technical curriculum

M.Tech in Structural Engineering (Part-time)

Part-Time Master of Technology in Structural Engineering

This part-time program is designed for professionals seeking expertise in structural analysis, earthquake-resistant design, concrete structures, and advanced construction technologies.

  • Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
  • Flexible schedule for working professionals
  • Focus on modern structural engineering concepts
  • Industry-oriented practical learning

M.Tech in Water Resources Engineering

Master of Technology in Water Resources Engineering

The program focuses on hydrology, irrigation engineering, water resource management, hydraulic structures, and sustainable water conservation techniques.

  • Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  • Advanced water resource management studies
  • Focus on irrigation and hydraulic engineering
  • Research and field-based learning

M.Tech in Water Resources Engineering (Part-time)

Part-Time Master of Technology in Water Resources Engineering

This part-time program is designed for working professionals interested in advanced knowledge of hydrology, water management systems, irrigation techniques, and sustainable resource planning.

  • Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
  • Flexible learning schedule
  • Focus on practical water management solutions
  • Industry and research-oriented curriculum

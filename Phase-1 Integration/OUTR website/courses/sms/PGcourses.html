MTech IEM

Master of Technology in Industrial Engineering and Management

The M.Tech IEM program focuses on industrial systems, production management, quality control, operations research, and optimization techniques for efficient industrial processes.

  • Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  • Focus on industrial operations and management systems
  • Research and industry-oriented practical learning
  • Training in production and quality management

MTech MSD

Master of Technology in Mechanical System Design

This program provides advanced knowledge in machine design, CAD/CAM, product development, manufacturing systems, and modern mechanical engineering technologies.

  • Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  • Advanced concepts in mechanical system design
  • Hands-on experience with modern design tools
  • Research and innovation-focused curriculum

MTech TE

Part-time Master of Technology in Thermal Engineering

The part-time M.Tech TE program focuses on thermodynamics, heat transfer, energy systems, fluid mechanics, and modern thermal engineering applications for working professionals.

  • Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
  • Flexible schedule for working professionals
  • Focus on thermal and energy engineering systems
  • Industry-oriented practical and research learning

MBA

Master of Business Administration

The MBA program develops leadership, management, communication, finance, and strategic decision-making skills for successful careers in business and corporate sectors.

  • Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  • Focus on business management and leadership
  • Industry exposure through projects and internships
  • Career opportunities in corporate and entrepreneurial sectors

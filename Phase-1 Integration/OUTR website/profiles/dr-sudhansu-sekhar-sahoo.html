About

Educational Qualifications:

• Ph.D. in Renewable Energy, IIT Bombay (QIP Fellowship, MHRD)

• M.Tech in Thermal Engineering, IIT Delhi (GATE Fellowship, MHRD)

• B.E. in Mechanical Engineering, UCE Burla (VSSUT)

Dr. Sudhansu Sekhar Sahoo is currently a Professor & Head in the School of Mechanical Sciences at Odisha University of Technology and Research (OUTR), Bhubaneswar. He was a recipient of the prestigious Bhaskara Advanced Solar Energy (BASE) Fellowship Program-2017, sponsored by the Department of Science and Technology, Govt. of India, and the Indo-U.S. Science and Technology Forum (IUSSTF), under which he conducted advanced research on thermal energy storage for solar thermal applications in the United States.

Dr. Asimananda Khandual

Assistant Professor & Head, Department of Textile Engineering

About

Dr. Asimananda Khandual is an Assistant Professor and the Head of the Department (HOD) of Textile Engineering at Odisha University of Technology and Research (OUTR), Bhubaneswar. With over two decades of distinguished academic, research, and collaborative experience, he plays a key role in driving innovation in Textile and Apparel Sciences. He is also recognized as an Innovation Ambassador by IIC-MHRD (Govt. of India) and serves as the SPOC for the ICT Sophisticated Lab at OUTR. His unique research profile bridges material chemistry, computational color science, and intelligent textile systems, leading to high-impact developments in smart wearable technology and the revitalization of traditional Indian handlooms like Sambalpuri Ikat.

Selected Publications & Books

• Applied Color Science for Textiles: Computational techniques using MATLAB (Springer, 2021) — A comprehensive textbook exploring computational models, spectral algorithms, and digital color formulation for textiles.

• Controlled nano-particle dyeing of cotton can ensure low cytotoxicity risk with multi-functional property enhancement (Materials Today Chemistry, 2020) — Highlights green nano-dyeing methods that optimize functional parameters while minimizing cell toxicity.

• Color and Texture Analysis of Textiles Using Image Acquisition and Spectral Analysis in Calibrated Sphere Imaging System-II (Electronics, 2023) — Focuses on digital color measurement algorithms and texture sorting for automated quality control.

• UVC LED and Conducting Yarn-Based Heater for a Smart Germicidal Face Mask to Protect against Airborne Viruses (Materials, 2021) — Demonstrates design and performance of smart personal protective equipment (PPE) incorporating thermal electro-conduction.

• Community, Local Practices and Cultural Sustainability: A Case Study of Sambalpuri Ikat Handloom (Book Chapter, Springer, 2020) — Documents local artisan socio-economic structures, manufacturing patterns, and cultural sustainability frameworks.

• Fashion Brands and Consumers Approach Towards Sustainable Fashion (Book Chapter, Springer, 2021) — Examines consumer psychology, eco-labeling, and corporate responsibility trends towards circular fashion economies.

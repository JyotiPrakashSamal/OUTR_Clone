About

Educational Qualifications:

Prof. (Dr.) Lokanath Tripathy is a Professor and the Head of the School of Electrical Sciences (formerly the Department of Electrical Engineering) at Odisha University of Technology and Research (OUTR), Bhubaneswar. With over 25 years of outstanding teaching experience in technical institutes and 4 years of key industry experience, he is a highly respected academic leader and researcher. He was honored with the prestigious POSOCO-2016 Award (Doctoral Category) by the Power System Operation Corporation, Government of India, in recognition of his groundbreaking doctoral research at IIT Bhubaneswar on FACTS-compensated transmission line protection. He has led various AICTE-sponsored research projects and continues to drive excellence in academic administration, teaching, and research.

Research Interests

Digital Protection of Power Systems Transmission Line Protection FACTS (Flexible AC Transmission Systems) Devices Smart Grids Digital Signal Processing in Power Systems

Key Achievements & Awards

• POSOCO Award (2016): Received the prestigious national award in the Doctoral Category from Power System Operation Corporation (POSOCO), Government of India, for his pioneering work on fault detection in FACTS-compensated transmission lines.

• Best Engineer Award (1997): Conferred by Petrofils Cooperatives Limited for outstanding contributions and professional engineering service.

• AICTE Sponsored Projects: Successfully led and completed multiple research projects funded by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), focusing on modern electrical systems protection.

• Distinguished Journal Reviewer: Serves as an active reviewer for leading international journals published by IEEE, IET, and Elsevier, contributing to the academic peer-review community.

• Research Publications: Published over 55 research papers in peer-reviewed international and national journals and conferences of high impact.

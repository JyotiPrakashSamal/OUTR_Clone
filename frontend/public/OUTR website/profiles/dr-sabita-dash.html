About

Educational Qualifications:

• Ph.D. in Civil Engineering, IIT Kharagpur (2024)

• M.Tech in Structural Engineering, NIT Rourkela (Gold Medalist, 2004)

• B.Tech in Civil Engineering, CET (now OUTR), Bhubaneswar

Dr. Sabita Dash is an Associate Professor and Head of the School of Infrastructure and Planning at Odisha University of Technology and Research (OUTR), Bhubaneswar. Joining the institution in January 2006, she has dedicated over 20 years to research, teaching, and administrative leadership. Her pioneering doctoral work at IIT Kharagpur focused on the eco-friendly characterization of concrete structural elements using sustainable alternatives like sea sand, contributing significantly to sustainable infrastructure and green concrete technology.

Research Interests

Sustainable Construction Materials Concrete Pavements Structural Engineering Reclaimed Asphalt Pavement (RAP) Sea Sand Aggregate Self-Healing Bio-Concrete Seismic Performance & Strengthening Machine Learning in Civil Engineering

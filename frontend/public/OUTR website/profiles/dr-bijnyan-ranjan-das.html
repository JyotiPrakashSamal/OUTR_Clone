Dr. Bijnyan Ranjan Das

Professor & Head, SBSH

About

Prof. (Dr.) Bijnyan Ranjan Das is a Professor and the Head of the School of Basic Sciences and Humanities (formerly the Department of Chemistry) at Odisha University of Technology and Research (OUTR), Bhubaneswar. With decades of rich academic and administrative experience, he drives teaching and research excellence in Physical and Materials Chemistry. He is deeply committed to students' welfare, having served in key administrative capacities, including Hostel Superintendent at CET/OUTR. As a senior academician, he actively contributes to the regional scientific community, serving in prestigious roles such as the Treasurer and Executive Member of the Orissa Chemical Society (OCS). His research outputs span premium journals and high-impact collaborations, fostering scientific innovation at OUTR.

Research Interests

Physical Chemistry Computational Chemistry Materials Chemistry Solvent-Solvent Molecular Interactions Ternary Liquid Mixtures Coal-Water Slurry Stabilization Green Synthesis of Nanoparticles Adsorption & Environmental Remediation

Selected Publications

• Comparative Study of Molecular Interaction in Ternary Liquid Mixtures of Polar and Non-Polar Solvents (Journal of Scientific Research, 2022) — Focuses on thermodynamic and acoustic dynamics in mixed solvent systems using ultrasonic and viscometric models.

• Rheological Characteristics and Stabilization of Coal-Water Slurry Using Sapindus laurifolia as a Natural Dispersant (ACS publications) — Investigates green surface-active agents to enhance slurry stability and optimize fuel characteristics.

• Adsorption of Fluoride and Congo Red Dye on Nanostructured Magnesium Oxide — Explores nanomaterial-based surface engineering for advanced environmental remediation and water treatment.

• Effect of Starch-Based Additives on the Stabilization and Viscous Behavior of Coal-Water Slurry Mixtures — Formulates polymeric slurry binders to improve viscosity, suspension stability, and energy transport efficiency.

Who We Are

A Legacy of Excellence
in Technical Education

Odisha University of Technology and Research (OUTR), formerly the College of Engineering and Technology (CET), is a premier state technical university established in 1981. Located in Bhubaneswar, the Silicon Valley of Odisha, OUTR stands as a beacon of technical and scientific education in Eastern India.

With eight specialized schools offering undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs, OUTR nurtures future engineers and researchers who are equipped to address the challenges of a rapidly evolving technological world.

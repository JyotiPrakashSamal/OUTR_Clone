About

Educational Qualifications:

• B.Tech. in Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering, CET (now OUTR), Bhubaneswar

Prof. (Dr.) Madhab Chandra Tripathy is a Professor and Head of the School of Electronic Sciences (formerly the Department of Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering) at Odisha University of Technology and Research (OUTR), Bhubaneswar. With over 18 years of outstanding teaching and research experience, his pioneering doctoral work at IIT Kharagpur on fractional-order systems received the prestigious INAE Innovative Doctoral Thesis Award. He has made significant contributions to the fields of analog circuit design, instrumentation systems, and biological system modeling, earning recognition globally as an accomplished academic leader and Senior Member of IEEE.

Research Interests

Fractional-Order Circuit Systems Analog Signal Processing Sensors & Instrumentation Systems Fractional Bioimpedance Modeling Fractional Inductor/Capacitor Realization Biological Systems Modeling
